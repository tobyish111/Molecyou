What is Molecyou?

Molecyou is an educational app that connects the activity, sleep, and fitness data on your iPhone to the molecular biology behind it, and lets you explore reference protein structures in interactive 3D. It's designed for learning — it does not provide medical advice.

Do I have to connect Apple Health?

No. Health access is entirely optional. You can explore everything using the built-in Demonstration Mode with sample data. If you do connect Health, Molecyou only requests read-only access and processes the data on your device.

How do I turn Health access on or off?

You can manage access anytime in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Health → Molecyou, or toggle Demonstration Mode from the Profile tab in the app.

Is my health data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your health data is processed only on your device and is never uploaded. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Where do the 3D protein structures come from?

Reference structures are retrieved from the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (EMBL-EBI). Only a public protein identifier is used to fetch a structure — no personal information is sent.

The app crashed or something looks wrong

Please email us at protein@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help.

Is Molecyou medical advice?

No. Molecyou provides educational information about human biology and reference protein structures. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it does not measure molecular activity inside your body. For medical questions, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
