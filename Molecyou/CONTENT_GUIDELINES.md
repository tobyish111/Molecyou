# Content Guidelines

Use cautious educational language.

Allowed:

- `Your recent activity makes oxygen transport an interesting system to explore.`
- `This protein is generally involved in muscle contraction.`
- `This is a reference structure from AlphaFold DB.`
- `Your health data remains on this device.`

Avoid:

- Claims that a protein increased, decreased, malfunctioned, folded incorrectly, or was measured.
- Diagnostic or therapeutic statements.
- Claims that AlphaFold analyzed the user's body.
- Medication efficacy claims.

Every new HealthKit mapping must point to educational relevance only.
