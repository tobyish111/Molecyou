# Molecyou

Molecyou is an iOS app that connects your fitness activity to the science of proteins. By reading your workout data, Molecyou identifies the proteins most relevant to your recent exercise — such as those involved in muscle contraction, repair, and energy metabolism — and then:

- **Summarizes** what those proteins do in plain language, giving you a quick and accessible overview of the biology behind your workout.
- **Visualizes** protein structures and activity in an interactive, engaging way so you can literally see the molecules that power your performance.
- **Educates** you about protein function, helping you understand how your body responds and adapts to different types of training over time.

Whether you are a casual runner or a dedicated athlete, Molecyou turns your workout history into a personalized biology lesson — bridging the gap between fitness tracking and molecular science.
